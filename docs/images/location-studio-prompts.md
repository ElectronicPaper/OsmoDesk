# Country and Shot Studio image prompts

Created on 2026-09-15 using the built-in image-generation tool, not the API/CLI.
No camera connection, paid AI Shot Copilot request, or physical recording was used.

## Reference roles and provenance

The country scenes used the earlier riverside equipment illustration for product
identity and the actual cinema screenshot for UI layout. All Niger sunset pixels
and source-photo credit claims were removed from these three new illustrations.
Their country views, on-screen pictures and scope traces are AI-generated, not
new Pocket sample photographs or app-calculated measurements.

The Studio scenes used the corrected Dolomites equipment illustration for camera
orientation and the actual Compose/Director screenshots for their screen contents.
Those captures show a synthetic three-position draft, not operator data.

Camera-head direction was corrected in all five location scenes. The three new
country scenes depict rear-facing gimbal heads aimed toward their subjects; the
earlier riverside and night-scene files were also corrected at the user's request.
Previous versions remain in Git history. Their existing photo attribution remains
required: see [sample credits](demo-samples/README.md).

All generated screens and hardware geometry remain illustrative, not pixel-exact
or hardware-verification evidence. The lens fronts need not be visible: the
operator viewpoint is behind the filming camera.
